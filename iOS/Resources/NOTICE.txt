MealTrace / 食迹 iOS. Original application code: MIT (repository LICENSE).
llama.cpp / GGML: MIT, pinned 547208dca250ceb3e4c8615598d043f2b602a527.
Native libraries are statically linked, with Metal shader source embedded.
Dependencies include stb (public domain / MIT), miniaudio (public domain / MIT),
xxHash (BSD), SHA-256 (Unlicense), rotate-bits (MIT), and Sheredom headers
(Unlicense). Original dependency license texts are supplied in Licenses.
No subprocess runner and no Google ML Kit are used in the iOS application.
Candidate image classification uses Apple's on-device Vision framework.

Vision weights: Qwen3.5-2B, Qwen team / Alibaba, Apache 2.0.
Source: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B
Unsloth GGUF revision f6d5376be1edb4d416d56da11e5397a961aca8ae.
Weights are downloaded or imported separately, with pinned SHA-256 verification.
This general-purpose model does not reliably measure food mass or hidden oil.

Nutrient data: USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy April 2018.
https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/download-datasets/
FDC IDs and original descriptions remain in foods.json, along with Chinese names.
The full 7,793-entry SR Legacy catalog is supplied. 113 entries have curated
Chinese names and aliases; other entries retain their original English names.
This remains an ingredient/reference catalog, not a complete Chinese recipe database.
Personal custom nutrient values are user-provided and are not USDA records.
